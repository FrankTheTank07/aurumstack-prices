Privacy Policy for AurumStack

Last updated: May 30, 2026

AurumStack is designed to help users track gold and silver holdings while keeping personal data private and stored locally.

Information We Collect

AurumStack does not require users to create an account and does not collect personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or financial account details.

Local Data Storage

Holdings, categories, notes, values, and related information entered into AurumStack are stored locally on the user's device. This information is not uploaded to servers owned or operated by AurumStack.

Market Pricing Data

AurumStack may retrieve publicly available precious metals pricing data to display estimated gold and silver values. When pricing data is requested, AurumStack does not send the user's holdings, notes, categories, or personal stack information.

Third-Party Services

AurumStack does not sell, share, or trade user information. The app may access externally hosted market pricing data, but no personal holdings data is transmitted as part of that request.

Analytics and Tracking

AurumStack does not use third-party analytics, advertising trackers, or behavior tracking tools.

Data Deletion

Because AurumStack stores data locally on the user's device, users can remove their data by deleting entries within the app or uninstalling the app from their device.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the developer through the support information provided with the app.
